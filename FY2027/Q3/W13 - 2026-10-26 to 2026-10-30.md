### Thursday

Had a page overnight for a pile of No Data alerts on fedstaging EC2 hosts. Just decom noise from the hosts going away, so I dropped it. Expect more of those until the Datadog monitors for fedstaging get removed from the chef repo.

Spent some time on the Cloudflare WARP case. Zack from support suggested some other app was overwriting DNS, so I went and checked what WARP actually binds. The macOS client only listens on the IPv4 proxy addresses 127.0.2.2 and .3, not the IPv6 ones their own client architecture docs say it binds. Ran the hotspot test on AT&T and AAAA worked immediately with nothing else changed, which killed the other-app theory. Cloudflare then confirmed in writing that the fix has to come in the client and there's no timeline. Sent Andrew an email asking them to prioritize it since it blocks us from deploying IPv6 resources in AWS.

Reviewed a few PRs in between. Jon's MAAT PR onboarding the core-infrastructure repos, where I asked for a config ordering fix and then approved, and two of Christopher's pd-runner-service-sre-cloudinfra PRs for the fedstaging runner decom and limiting the fedprod deploy pipeline to main.
